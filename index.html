The workbook is a public bank of exercises, published on this site and updated each week.

What You Will Learn

By the end of the semester you will be able to explain what happens inside an LLM, build a working system around one, and choose between prompting, retrieval, fine-tuning and agents on evidence rather than fashion.

  • Explain the model — tokens, embeddings, attention, the transformer, and the three training stages.
  • Choose the right tool — prompting, PEFT, RAG, or an agent, and defend the choice on cost, latency and accuracy.
  • Build and evaluate a system on top of a pretrained model — the job almost everyone in the industry actually has — and know what it costs to run.
  • Judge failure and risk — how these systems break, and what guardrails are worth putting on them.
