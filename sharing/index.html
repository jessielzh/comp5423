AI is a paradigm shift, for what we teach and for how we teach it. PolyU Education 4.0 (E4.0) initiative calls for an AI-empowered, student-centred learning and teaching model that envisions a future where technology supports interactive, flexible, and personalised learning. This COMP5423 course is one pilot toward that vision: this term the whole course was redesigned from scratch with an AI agent.

Interactive

How do you make a 3-hour class of 100 students interactive?

Step 1 · LectureTeaching with web pages instead of PowerPoint.

Slides you click, not just watch.

Step 2 · PracticeLearning by doing.

An AI-made workbook is a personal tutor.

Step 3 · ReviewReinforce the teaching with the hard questions.

Class data decides what to review.

Flexible

Learning does not stop at the classroom door.

The slides and the workbook are plain web pages. They open on a laptop or a phone, in class or on the bus, with nothing to install.

Slides open anywhere, anytime.

Practice anywhere, anytime.

Hands-on

From learner to creator.

Training an LLM from scratch: every student builds a small GPT and trains it on 24,000+ Tang poems, until it writes poems of its own.
